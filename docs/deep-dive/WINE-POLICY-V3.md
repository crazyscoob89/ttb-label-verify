# Additive wine comparison policy — revision 3

`web/lib/wine-rules.ts` exports **`compareApplicationV3(application: Application, evidence: ExtractionEvidence): ComparisonResultV3`**, **`RULES_REVISION_V3 = 3`**, and the **`ComparisonResultV3`** type. The result is the existing discriminated comparison shape: unchanged failure variant, or complete seven-field result with required `rulesRevision: 3` and unchanged `rulesVersion: 'prototype-seven-fields-v1'`. It delegates runtime validation and baseline decisions to frozen revision 2 (`rules.ts`), preserves raw expected/observed data and physical-size finding, and never mutates caller inputs. No provider, image access, network, persistence or Pass decision is performed.

## Deliberate changes

- **Broad wine class:** only commodity `wine` with generic declared `Wine` can recognize a whole readable value in this bounded list: Cabernet Sauvignon, Chardonnay, Merlot, Pinot Noir, Sauvignon Blanc, Syrah, Riesling, Zinfandel. Ordinary NFC/case/layout equivalence applies. The result says **broad-class compatible**, explicitly **not evidence of varietal percentage or subtype regulatory requirements**. Specific grape conflicts remain mismatches. No substring, blend, suffix, fuzzy spelling or regulatory certification is inferred. Values outside the list retain revision-2 behavior; uncertain evidence stays review.
- **Domestic wine location:** only readable whole `Lodi`, `Napa`, or `Sonoma` paired with `CA` or `California`, separated by whitespace and an optional comma, is recognized. These are geographic producer locations, not foreign-country evidence: imported-country comparison is `not-applicable`, not a verified country match. No domestic country statement requirement is introduced. No grape origin or producer-address accuracy is certified. Arbitrary city names, state-only text, prose, prefixes and mixed locations retain revision-2 review behavior; recognized foreign-country contradictions (e.g. `PRODUCT OF NEW ZEALAND`) remain mismatches. Imported origin behavior is unchanged, including missing-country review. Existing intake already accepts US/USA/period variants and full United States names; no schema or alias change was needed.
- **Warning explanations, all commodities:** preserve revision-2 status **exactly**, including known-defect precedence over uncertainty. Only layout whitespace is normalized; case, punctuation, Unicode distinctions and hyphenation are not waived. Reasons separate heading wording/capitalization/punctuation, body wording, heading boldness, body boldness, and unverified physical print size. Text differences quote reference and raw observed text and give deterministic exact-token missing/unexpected spans with one-based positions (punctuation remains attached to words). This diff is diagnostic, never a fuzzy acceptance rule. Missing/uncertain/unreadable text does not generate a claimed wording defect. Matching wording plus unknown boldness is review, not a wording mismatch; known bad boldness is a formatting mismatch. The reference remains the existing fixed `WARNING_REFERENCE`; no legal rules were added.
